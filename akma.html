Taharat (Paaki)

💧 Wuzu Ke 4 Farz

In 4 jagahon mein ek baal barabar hissa bhi sookha rahe toh Wuzu adhura rehta hai:

  • 1. Pura Chehra Dhona: Peshani ke baalon se thodi ke niche tak, aur ek kaan ki lau se dusre kaan tak.
  • 2. Dono Haath Kohniyon Samet: Ungliyon se lekar kohni (elbows) samet dhona.
  • 3. Sar Ka Masah: Chauthai (1/4) sar par bheega haath pherna.
  • 4. Dono Paon Takhno Samet: Ungliyon se lekar takhno (ankles) ke upar tak dhona.
Namaz Se Pehle

🕌 Namaz Ki 7 Shartein

Namaz shuru karne se pehle in 7 baton ka hona Farz hai:

  • 1. Badan ka paak hona (Wuzu/Ghusl).
  • 2. Kapde ka napaaki se paak hona.
  • 3. Namaz padhne ki jagah ka paak hona.
  • 4. Satar ka dhaka hona.
  • 5. Namaz ka sahi waqt hona.
  • 6. Qibla (Kaaba) ki taraf rukh hona.
  • 7. Dil mein namaz ki Niyat hona.
Namaz Ke Andar

✨ Namaz Ke 6 Faraiz (Arkaan)

Namaz ke andar ke 6 laazmi hisse:

  • 1. Takbeer-e-Tahreema: Shuru mein 'Allahu Akbar' kehna.
  • 2. Qiyam: Seedhe khade hona.
  • 3. Qira'at: Quran ki aayatein padhna.
  • 4. Ruku: Ghutno par haath rakh kar jhukna.
  • 5. Sujood: Har rakat mein do sajde karna.
  • 6. Qa'dah Akheerah: Aakhiri baithak mein Attahiyyat ki miqdar baithna.

💡 2 Rakat, 3 Rakat aur 4 Rakat Mein Kya Farq Hota Hai? (Zaroori Yaad Rakhein)

📌 Farz Namaz Ki 3ri aur 4thi Rakat: Zohar, Asr, Maghrib aur Isha ke Farz mein pehli 2 rakat ke baad Attahiyyat padh kar jab 3ri rakat ke liye uthte hain, toh 3ri aur 4thi rakat mein sirf Bismillah aur Surah Fatiha padhi jaati hai (alag se Surah nahi milayi jaati).
📌 Sunnat, Nafl aur Witr Ki Har Rakat: 4 Rakat Sunnat, Nafl ya 3 Rakat Witr ki har rakat (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th) mein Surah Fatiha ke baad koi ek Surah milana zaroori hai.
